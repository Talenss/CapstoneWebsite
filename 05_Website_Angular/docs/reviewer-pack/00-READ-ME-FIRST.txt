WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY IT IS SHORTER
───────────────────────────────────

  The old pack was eleven files and 81,000 words. It is now five files and
  50,000, and nothing was thrown away. The difference was repetition: the
  same ten or fifteen facts were re-explained in up to eleven places, and
  the same questions were answered in four.

  That was not only long, it had gone wrong. Two of the copies had drifted
  into contradicting the paper:

    · One told you to say the sample size of forty "comes from a power
      calculation". The paper says in as many words that it is NOT a power
      calculation. If a panelist reads both, that is a bad moment.

    · Six files promised respondents anonymity. The form as administered
      asks for an email address, so the responses are confidential, not
      anonymous.

  Both are corrected. Numbers now live in ONE file, so the next correction
  is one edit and not nine.

THE THREE ANSWERS EVERYONE MUST KNOW
────────────────────────────────────

  1. IS THIS AI?  No. Scoring, ranking and recommending are a fixed sum you
     can check on paper. AI appears in one optional place: reading a file
     an applicant chose to upload, so the builder can be pre-filled. It
     never scores anybody. "The AI fills in boxes, it does not judge."

  2. WHERE DID 40 COME FROM?  A practical minimum for a single-site
     descriptive study, stated as a judgement. NOT a formula and NOT a
     power calculation. Slovin does not apply: the sampling is purposive
     and there is no list to draw randomly from. What we achieved is 103
     valid responses, 46 of them matching the population the study defines.

  3. WHY THESE TOOLS?  Angular and Node are one language, so the scoring
     sum in the browser and the one on the server are provably the same —
     and a test compares 500 generated pairs to prove it. PostgreSQL
     enforces our rules itself.


THE SHORT VERSION, IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE
───────────────────────────────────────────

  Waterfall built the app. Quantitative descriptive is the study.
  The score is arithmetic. Anyone can check it on paper.
  BOTH resumes are scored. Only the built one decides position.
  The AI only reads UPLOADED FILES, and never scores anybody.
  The system never rejects anyone. A person always decides.

  Skills 50, education 25, experience 25.
  Strong is 80 and up. Possible is 50 to 79. Related is below 50.
  A skill you cannot say where you used counts half, never zero.
  The applicant is told HOW MANY are missing, never which.

  198 automated tests across eight suites, all passing.
  103 valid survey responses; 46 match the stated population.
  All available HR staff, about ten. Plus five IT experts, three the floor.
  Users rate three ISO characteristics; the experts rate five.

  And when you do not know an answer:
  "I do not know that. What I would do is ____, and I can tell you
   by ____." Then write it down in front of them.
