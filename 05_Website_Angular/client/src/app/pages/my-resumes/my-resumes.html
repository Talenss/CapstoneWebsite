What “active” decides

The active resume is the one scored against every opening on your dashboard, and the one attached when you apply. Changing it re-scores everything straight away. A resume already sent with an application is never altered by this — the employer keeps the version you actually sent.

Why keep more than one

Each opening scores what it asks for.

A resume written for a support role and one written for a technical role will score differently against the same two openings, because they lead with different skills. Keeping one per kind of role is usually worth more than one long resume trying to cover everything.
