# ResumeMatch PH — crawler rules
#
# The four public pages are the whole indexable site: the home page, the
# openings list, the learning hub and the privacy notice. Everything else is
# behind a login and would show a crawler nothing but a redirect to /auth.
#
# Note on Disallow vs noindex: the rules below stop the crawl, which is what
# saves crawl budget. They do NOT remove a page already in the index — a
# disallowed page can never be re-crawled, so its noindex is never seen. The
# pages themselves also send `robots: noindex, nofollow` (see app.routes.ts),
# and that is the directive that actually removes them.

User-agent: *
