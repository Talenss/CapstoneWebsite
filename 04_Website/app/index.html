How it works

One profile. Four steps.

You fill in your details once. Everything after that is automatic — except the decision, which stays human.

Build it, step by step

A guided wizard walks you through each section, with writing tips and ATS formatting guidance built in — so a first resume doesn't need a template you found somewhere else. Attach your own resume file too, if you already have one.

See your real score

The engine reads the structured data you entered and compares it against each opening's stated requirements: skills at 50%, education at 25%, experience at 25%. You get a number and the full breakdown behind it.

Apply, and follow it

Apply in one click, then watch it move: Submitted → Under review → Shortlisted → Hired. Or Not selected — decided by a person at the hotel, never by the software.

A recruiter reads it

Royce's HR sees a ranked list with every score broken down, alongside both your system-built resume and the file you uploaded. The ranking is a starting point for their judgment, not a substitute for it.

The two-resume model

A machine reads one. A human reads both.

Automated screening fails people when a parser misreads their formatting. So we don't parse files at all — we score only what you typed in yourself, and hand your actual document straight to the recruiter.

The structured resume

Scored. Built in the wizard, field by field. Because you entered it directly, nothing gets lost to a parsing error — the engine reads exactly what you meant to say.

+

Your own file

Never scored. Optional PDF, Word, or image. It goes to the recruiter for review and verification — read by a person, weighed by a person, and never touched by the matching engine.
