Who you are

This is the header of your resume. Keep the summary to two or three plain sentences — what you studied, what you've done, what you're looking for.

Write it in plain sentences. Skip words like "hardworking" — say what you actually did instead.

Education

The level you pick here is a quarter of every compatibility score, so choose the one that matches the diploma you actually hold.

Experience

Internships, OJT, part-time work, and student-organisation roles all count. If you have none, skip this — plenty of openings ask for zero months.

Skills

Half of every score comes from this one list. Be specific — "MySQL" scores; "computer skills" doesn't match anything.

Skills Royce is currently asking for

Certifications

TESDA certificates, short courses, seminars, training. Optional — but they're free evidence, so add them if you have them.
